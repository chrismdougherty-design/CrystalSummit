## Overview

Crystal Summit is a fantasy environment centered around ascending a small mountain by following a river upstream toward a giant crystal at the summit.

The environment combines natural scenery with magical crystal formations that have spread throughout the landscape. Crystals grow from trees, plants, cliffs, bridges, torii gates, and other structures, while others float freely in the air.

The overall goal is to create a peaceful but mysterious environment through shader development, lighting, and environmental design.

## Visual Direction

My main inspiration comes from the **Tree of Beginning** in *Pokémon: Lucario and the Mystery of Mew*, particularly its crystal formations and the idea of a living ecosystem.

Additional inspirations include:

- **Avatar:** Bioluminescent vegetation and glowing natural environments.
- **Shadow of the Colossus:** Environmental scale and massive distant creatures.

The intended color palette focuses on teal, cyan, blue, and purple, with sunset or nighttime lighting to emphasize the glowing elements.

## Shader Focus

The project will explore several material and shader techniques:

| Feature | Intended Effect |
|---|---|
| Crystal Materials | Emissive glow, transparency, and subtle pulsing |
| River and Waterfall | Animated water, distortion, and reflections |
| Fantasy Vegetation | Bioluminescent details and foliage movement |
| Crystal Growth | Blending crystal formations with environmental objects |
| Atmosphere | Fog, lighting, and environmental depth |

## Project Setup & Version Control

The project is designed for Unreal Engine 5.8.2, using a basic environment layout with landscape terrain and starter meshes as the foundation for future shader development.

GitHub is used for version control to track changes to the environment, materials, and project files throughout development.

The repository includes an Unreal Engine `.gitignore` configuration to exclude unnecessary generated files, such as `Intermediate`, `Saved`, and `DerivedDataCache`, while retaining the project configuration and content files.
